\begin{center}
    \textbf{\large ABSTRACT}
\end{center}

\vspace{14pt}

\begin{singlespace}
\noindent\justifying
Information about lost animals is often spread across social media posts, messaging groups and printed posters, making it difficult to gather information and follow up on a search. This study presents WeFIND, a collaborative mobile application that centralizes reports of lost and found animals and organizes community participation based on each case's location. The applied research combined a questionnaire with pet owners and animal protectors with a comparative analysis of similar platforms. The results identified posts losing visibility and information being scattered across several groups as the main difficulties, as well as concerns about exposing personal phone numbers and interest in viewing cases on a map. Based on these findings, the system requirements and UML models were defined. The application was developed iteratively and incrementally using React Native and Expo, with Supabase and a PostgreSQL database for authentication, storage and real-time synchronization. It allows users to publish and browse reports in a list and on a map, filter them by proximity and animal characteristics, register sightings linked to each search, chat privately and generate QR-code posters. As a distinguishing feature, WeFIND includes a heuristic matching mechanism that compares lost and found reports based on species, physical characteristics and geographic distance, suggesting possible reunions for users to confirm. Tests with fictitious reports identified both correct matches and false positives, highlighting the need for users to confirm the suggestions. A usability evaluation with pet owners, based on the System Usability Scale (SUS), indicated a positive perception of ease of use and also identified opportunities to improve the interface. It is concluded that WeFIND brings together, in a single tool, features that were not found in an integrated way in the analyzed platforms, helping make the search for lost animals more organized and collaborative.

\noindent
\textbf{Keywords:} Lost animals. Mobile applications. Geolocation. Collaborative systems. Heuristic matching.
\end{singlespace}
